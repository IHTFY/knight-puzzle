# Alpha chess pieces

The original Alpha pieces by Eric Bentzen are included unchanged in
`images/pieces/` (wN, bQ, and the hidden wP drag placeholder).

Source: https://lichess1.org/assets/_v88h3i/piece/alpha/{piece}.svg

Lichess attributes this set to Eric Bentzen and lists its terms as
"free for personal non commercial use":
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md

Original distribution: http://www.enpassant.dk/chess/downl/alpha.zip
